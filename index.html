03 — Study 1 of 1
Study 01

Leader Credibility & Public Approval

Study 1 tests whether a country leader failing to follow through on a threat reduces public support for the leader, because the public could punish leaders either for revealing their belligerence or for inconsistency between their statements and behaviors. This experiment presents respondents with a hypothetical scenario regarding each country's relations with other countries around the world. After reviewing the text, respondents evaluate whether they approve or disapprove of the country leader on a 7-point scale.

Experiment Design — US Version
Instructions

We will now present to you a hypothetical scenario regarding US relations with other countries around the world. You will read about a situation that many countries have faced in the past and will probably face again in the future. Different leaders have handled such situations in different ways. We will describe one approach that leaders have taken, and ask whether you approve or disapprove of the approach.

Imagine that a country sent its military to take over some of its neighbor's territory.

Control Condition

The US's country leader announced that the US would stay out of the conflict. The attacking country continued to invade and the conflict ended with the attacking country taking control of 20% of the contested territory.

Treatment Condition

The US's country leader announced that if the attacking country continued to invade, the US military would immediately engage and attempt to push out the attacking country. The US sent troops to the region. The attacking country continued to invade. The US ordered the US military not to engage. The attacking country continued to invade and the conflict ended with the attacking country taking control of 20% of the contested territory.

Outcome Measure

How much would you approve or disapprove of the way the US's country leader handled the situation?

1 = definitely disapprove  ·  7 = definitely approve
